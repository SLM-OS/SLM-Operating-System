What the mtimes mean: SLM-OS builds FatFs with no clock. kernel/lib/fatfs/ffconf.h:111-114 sets FF_FS_NORTC 1 and
FF_NORTC_YEAR/MON/MDAY 2026/4/25, and kernel/lib/fatfs/ff.c:274 builds every timestamp from those (get_fattime in
kernel/lib/fatfs/diskio.c:203 is never called in that mode, :197-198). Every file SLM-OS creates or rewrites is therefore
stamped 2026-04-25T00:00:00Z, at any boot. The mtimes cannot show whether a file was written today. Files stamped
2026-04-27T09:25:48Z were written by something with a clock (the host, when the card was prepared).
